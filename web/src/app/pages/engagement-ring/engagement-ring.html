Table %

The table is the flat facet on the top of a diamond. Table % compares its width to the diamond's overall width. Different table proportions can affect how a diamond reflects light; commonly referenced ranges (for example, roughly 54–62% on round diamonds) are general guidance rather than a strict rule, and can vary by shape.

Depth %

Depth % compares a diamond's total height to its width. Proportions that are unusually shallow or deep may affect how light returns through the top of the stone. Commonly referenced ranges (for example, roughly 59–63% on round diamonds) are general guidance rather than a strict rule, and can vary by shape.

Length / Width Ratio

Length/Width Ratio compares a diamond's length to its width. A ratio near 1.00 generally corresponds to more square or round proportions, while a higher ratio describes a more elongated shape. This is most relevant for fancy shapes such as Oval, Pear, Marquise, and Emerald, and preferred proportions are largely a matter of personal taste.
